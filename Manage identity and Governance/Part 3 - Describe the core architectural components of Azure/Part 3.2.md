# 🚀 Getting Started with Azure Accounts

## 🧾 Azure Subscription Basics

- To use Azure services, you need an **Azure subscription**.
- Subscriptions are created when you sign up for an **Azure account**.
- You can have **multiple subscriptions** under one account (e.g., for dev, sales, marketing).

---

## 🆓 Azure Free Account

- **12 months** of free access to popular services  
- **$200 credit** for the first 30 days  
- **25+ services** always free  
- Requires: phone number, credit card (for verification), Microsoft/GitHub account  
- No charges until you upgrade

---

## 🎓 Azure Free Student Account

- Includes **$100 credit**  
- Free developer tools  
- No credit card required  

---

## 🧪 Microsoft Learn Sandbox

- Used during Learn modules  
- Provides a **temporary subscription** for hands-on practice  
- Automatically cleans up resources after the module ends  
- Preferred for learning – no cost, no impact on your real subscription
